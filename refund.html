Refund Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

We want practices to feel confident trying Cadence. This policy explains how refunds work.

1. Free trial

New accounts include a free trial period. No payment is collected until the trial ends and the practice chooses to continue.

2. Monthly subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed in advance for each monthly period. If a request for a refund is made within 7 days of a charge, we will refund that charge in full. After 7 days, charges for the current billing period are non-refundable, but cancellation will stop any future billing.

3. Annual subscriptions

Where an annual plan is offered, a prorated refund for unused months may be issued if requested within 30 days of the annual charge.

4. Billing errors

If a practice is charged in error — for example, after a cancellation that did not process correctly — we will refund the incorrect charge in full once verified.

5. How to request a refund

Send a request to billing@cadence-app.example with the account email and the charge date. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
