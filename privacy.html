Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cadence ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when a practice and its clients use our booking, notes, and billing service.

1. Information we collect

We collect account information from the practitioner or clinic administrator who signs up (name, email, business details), and client information entered into the platform by the practice, which may include contact details, appointment history, and session notes.

2. How client health information is handled

  • Session notes and intake forms are encrypted both in transit and at rest.
  • Access to client records is restricted to the staff the practice authorizes, and every access is logged.
  • We do not sell client data, and we do not use client health information for advertising.

3. How we use information

Information is used to operate the booking, notes, and billing features of Cadence, to send appointment reminders on the practice's behalf, and to process payments through our payment partners.

4. Data sharing

We share data only with the sub-processors needed to run the service — such as hosting and payment infrastructure providers — under agreements that require them to protect the data to the same standard.

5. Data retention

Client records are retained for as long as the practice's account is active. A practice may request export or deletion of its data at any time, subject to any legal retention requirements applicable to health records in their jurisdiction.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Requests can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@cadence-app.example.
