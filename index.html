Questions

Before you start a trial

Is client data encrypted?+

Yes. Session notes, intake forms and client records are encrypted both in transit and at rest, and every access to a record is logged.

Can I switch plans later?+

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from account settings. The change applies to your next billing cycle.

Do you charge per client?+

No. Every plan includes unlimited clients — pricing is based on how many practitioners on your team need a login.

What happens to my data if I cancel?+

You can export all client records and notes within 30 days of cancelling. After that period, the data is deleted from our systems.

Is there a setup fee?+

No setup fee. Start on a free trial, and only the monthly plan price applies once you decide to continue.
